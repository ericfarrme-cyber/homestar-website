## Caption

Putting a sink in a basement is not a plumbing detail. It is a decision about the floor.

This Westfield basement had 1,754 unfinished square feet and no drain line under the slab. So
before any of this existed, we saw-cut about fifteen feet of concrete, trenched a new line to a
sewage ejector basin in the storage room, and repaired the floor flush. Everything you see here
sits on top of that one day of noise and dust.

What it became: a wet bar with quartz counters, stacked gloss tile to the ceiling and a
full-height refrigerator. A black built-in running nearly fifteen feet around a linear fireplace.
A guest bedroom, an exercise room, finished storage, and a full bath with a fluted white oak
double vanity and a tiled shower.

And under the stairs, a door with a cedar awning and a window box, because the kids were going to
claim that space anyway.

Roughly ten to twelve weeks, start to finish.
