**Why this review and not another.** Pulled from the live Google feed on the site on
2026-09-06, quoted verbatim. It was chosen because it is *specific* — the client
portal is a real differentiator the site already advertises, and a review corroborating
it is worth more than praise about quality, which every contractor's reviews contain.
It also names Eric and Robb, which reinforces the owner-run positioning, and lands on
communication, the thing homeowners most expect a contractor to get wrong.

Runners-up, both usable later: **Sheila Pierson** on the Westfield basement ("what was
once an unfinished storage space") — held back because Westfield already carries the

the best whole-home argument in the set, worth its own post rather than a caption slot.

**Careful with the rating claim.** The card and caption say 5.0 and 85 reviews, which
is what Google displays and what the site's structured data publishes. Do not upgrade
that to "not one below five stars" — a 5.0 can be a rounded 4.95, and the reviews were
not audited one by one.
