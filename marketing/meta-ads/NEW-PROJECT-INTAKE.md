# New project intake — getting fresh work into the calendar

**The problem this solves.** The six-week plan leans on reserve assets by weeks 5 and 6 (FH, FJ, F5)
and three Sunday carousels are unbuilt. Meanwhile jobs are finishing every month. A finished job
photographed this week is a better post than a reserve cut of a job from spring — it is newer, it
has before shots, and the crew still remembers the details worth writing about.

Without a standing process, new work does not reach the calendar because nobody's job it is to move
it. This is that job, written down.

## What Eric sends, when a job wraps

Drop into `public/images/` using the existing naming pattern — `town-project-N.jpg`, lowercase,
hyphenated, numbered. Then say in one line:

1. **Town** — Fishers, Carmel, Zionsville, Noblesville, Westfield, Geist
2. **Rooms** — bathroom / kitchen / basement / whole-home / other
3. **Rough scope** — one sentence. What was the interesting problem?
4. **Before shots?** — yes or no. This decides whether it can be a before/after, which is the
   strongest format we have.
5. **Video?** — walkthrough clips, even phone footage, make a reel possible

That is all. The assessment is not Eric's job.

## What happens to it

Every new project is scored against the gaps in the current plan, not against how nice it looks.
As of 2026-09-07 the gaps are, in priority order:

| gap | why it matters | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Kitchens** | Zero kitchens across 12 reels and 24 stories, and kitchen is the weakest AI category at 4/6 | AI sweep 2026-09-07 |
| **Carmel and Noblesville** | 4 mentions each across six weeks, against Zionsville 12 and Geist 11 | town tally |
| **Design-build / whole-home framing** | HomeStar absent from "design build firm" queries; Nicholas Design Build owns them | AI sweep 2026-09-07 |
| **Before shots** | The strongest reel format, and the reserve has none left | — |

A new **Carmel kitchen with before shots** would be the single most valuable thing to arrive. A
seventh Zionsville bathroom would be the least, however good it looks.

## Where new work goes in the calendar

**Rule: new work displaces the reserve, never a queued post.**

- Reels are queued and caption-verified through **2 October**. Do not disturb those — rescheduling
  is possible but editing a queued reel's caption is awkward, because reels store text as
  `description` on the video object rather than `message` on the post.
- Weeks 5 and 6 (5–18 Oct) are the landing zone. Those slots currently hold FM, FP, FL and FF, and
  the story grid there leans on repeat towns.
- The three unbuilt Sunday carousels — 27 Sep, 11 Oct, 18 Oct — are the easiest slots to fill with
  a new project, because nothing has been built for them yet.

## The bar

Not every finished job is a post. It needs at least one of:

- a genuine **before** that makes the after mean something
- a **detail worth a sentence** — a drain centred on a pattern, a mantle cut to one wall, tile
  where no two pieces match
- a **room type or town the plan is short of** (see the gap table)

A well-executed but unremarkable bathroom in a town already covered three times is a portfolio
photo, not a post. Say so rather than scheduling it.

## Cadence

Asked every Monday as part of the daily reminder, alongside Houzz. One question: did anything wrap
last week. If yes, this document runs. If no, it costs a sentence.
