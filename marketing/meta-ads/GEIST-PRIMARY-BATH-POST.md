## Caption — copy everything below the line

From the vanity, all you used to see was a door.

Behind it, the toilet and a framed shower stall shared a small closed-off room at the far end of this Geist primary bath. The tub sat outside it under the window, with a full wall between the two.

That wall is now a half wall - tiled, then capped in frameless glass. The shower, the tub and the window finally read as one room.

Out: a jetted tub in almond tile, a four-inch tile stall behind a frosted framed door, a one-piece vanity top with molded bowls, a single wall-to-wall mirror, and sage green.

In: a drop-in soaking tub in a tiled deck on a wall-mounted filler, glazed handmade-look tile laid vertically through the shower and up the tub surround, a round mosaic shower floor, a two-tier niche, a stained double vanity with a veined white stone top, two wood-framed mirrors, and warm white walls.

Two details worth zooming in on. The old tub's wood front panel couldn't be saved when the jetted tub came out, so the new deck is tiled to match the surround. And inside the shower there's a small shave-shelf niche framed into the knee wall, about fifteen inches off the floor.

Under the tile, every wet surface is on the complete Schluter system - Kerdi on the shower walls, Ditra on the floor - and all plumbing was done by a licensed plumber. Schluter's 25-year manufacturer warranty covers the waterproofing, and our own 1-year workmanship warranty covers the job.
