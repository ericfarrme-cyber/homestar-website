reels queued through 2 October were therefore queued on the Page only, and Instagram got
nothing. F7 was published to Instagram by hand on 2026-09-07 and is live at
https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dc_MZrPAFAv/ - the seven below are still outstanding.

Each reel goes up at **09:00**, matching its Facebook twin. Captions are reproduced from
the files the Facebook posts were built from, so the two platforms read identically.

**In Business Suite: turn the Facebook Page OFF in 'Post to'.** The composer defaults to
both, and Facebook already has every one of these scheduled - leaving it on double-posts.
