## Caption — copy everything below the line

Green tile is not a safe choice. That was the point.

This Carmel homeowner didn't want the bathroom their neighbors had. They wanted one decision that was unmistakably theirs, and then everything else in the room got designed around it - the custom vanity and stone top, the brass, the black-and-white basketweave shower pan, the arched mirror, the shutters that keep the light soft on the glaze.

The tile runs full height in the shower and carries around the rest of the room as a wainscot, so the color reads from every angle instead of sitting on one feature wall.

The part you can't see is the part that decides whether it lasts. Ditra on the floor, Kerdi on the walls - the complete Schluter system, waterproofed before a single tile was set. 25-year warranty, standard, not an upgrade.

## Why this copy

Every visual detail named in the caption was checked against the footage before
it was written - the wainscot line, the floor-mounted brass filler, the
basketweave pan, the arched mirror, the plantation shutters. The vanity is
called "custom" rather than "walnut": the project page says custom, and the
species is not identifiable from 720p footage.

No before-and-after framing, deliberately. The Fishers caption could run an
"out / in" structure because that Reel was about a layout that stayed put. There
is no Carmel before footage, so promising a transformation the video does not
show would be a bait.

No hashtags and plain hyphens, matching the earlier Reels. No pricing in the
caption - the end card carries the band, and a comment thread is a poor place to
defend a number.
