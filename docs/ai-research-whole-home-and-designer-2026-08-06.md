## Finding 1 — "Design-build" is the gatekeeper term for whole-home, and we don't hold it

On the highest-intent whole-home query, ChatGPT opened its answer with this:

> *"For a whole-home renovation in Hamilton County, I'd focus on **design-build firms** rather than hiring separate architects and contractors. A design-build company manages design, budgeting, permits, construction, and subcontractors under one contract, which tends to reduce surprises on large remodels."*

Then it named: Everything Home, The HomeWright, **Bender Homes**, **Benjamin Design Build**, Custom Home Medic, MJ Brown, Home Redemption, Absolute Renovations, **Nicholas Design Build**, **Form Building Company**.

HomeStar was not among them. The filter was not quality or reviews — it was **category membership**. ChatGPT decided whole-home belongs to design-build firms and then listed design-build firms.

**We meet that definition and never say so.** We have in-house design, 3D renderings before demolition, permits, all licensed trades on our own payroll, and one contract. That *is* design-build. Two of the firms beating us have "Design Build" literally in their business name.

**This is the single highest-leverage change available for whole-home.** Not more content — the *term*.

## Finding 2 — We rank for designer collaboration, but with no collaboration language attached

On "contractor who works with interior designers in Fishers Indiana" we placed **#3**. But compare the descriptions ChatGPT gave:

| Firm | How ChatGPT described them |
|---|---|
| Nicholas Design Build | "regularly coordinate design, estimating, and construction… **a good fit if you're working with an independent interior designer**" |
| Everything Home | "design-build firm with interior designers, project managers…" |
| Custom Living | "**also works alongside outside designers when clients already have one**" |
| **HomeStar** | "known for kitchens, bathrooms, trim work, built-ins, and coordinating licensed trades" |

Everyone else got collaboration framing. We got a service list. We are in the room and not described as doing the thing the query asked about.

ChatGPT even articulated the exact positioning our new page argues:

> *"a general contractor who is comfortable collaborating with an interior designer (**rather than trying to replace one**)"*
> *"If you already have an interior designer and just need a builder who will execute the plans, these contractors are generally a better fit than firms that insist on using only their own designers."*

That is our thesis, written by the engine, awarded to competitors. `/working-with-your-designer` went live today; this is the baseline it should move.

## Finding 3 — Dovetail appears in the same answers we do, and the link between us is invisible

In that same result, **Dovetail Group was in the map pack** (categorised "Interior designer") and named in the body — recommended to designers looking for contractor partners. Our three completed projects together were nowhere.

The association exists in reality and on our website. It does not exist anywhere the model is retrieving from. Fixing that is a **third-party** problem, not a content problem:
- A reciprocal link/mention from Dovetail's own site is worth more here than anything we publish about them
- Houzz project credits naming both firms
- Any local press or directory listing that names the partnership

## Finding 4 — Query intent splits hard, and half our new FAQs target the wrong side

| Pattern | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| "**best / who should I hire** … + city" | Names businesses. Map pack. **Commercially valuable.** |
| "**should I / do / what's the difference**" | Generic advice, **zero businesses named** — even when a city is included |

"Do remodeling contractors work with outside interior designers" and "should I hire a designer or contractor first" both returned pure explainer content with no company named at all.

Roughly half the FAQs on the new designer page target that second type. They still earn topical authority and help real humans, but **they will not produce a citation**. The FAQs that can win are the localized, discovery-shaped ones.

## Finding 5 — We are being tiered as mid-market on whole-home

ChatGPT's own budget guidance from run 1:

> Luxury ($300k+): **Everything Home, William Gordon Group**
> Upper mid-range ($150k–300k): MJ Brown, The HomeWright
> Mid-range ($75k–200k): **HomeStar Services**, Home Redemption

We are being sorted below the firms we most want to compete with on whole-home. Our largest published project is the ~$150,000 Westfield basement; we publish no whole-home figure at all. The model tiered us on the evidence available, and the evidence caps out at $150K.

**This is what the missing whole-home price floor costs us** — not just a lost citation, but a lower tier placement.

## Finding 6 — Competitor set keeps expanding

New this run: **William Gordon Group** (boutique design-build, older homes, complex reconfigurations), **Bender Homes**, **Benjamin Design Build**, **MJ Brown Renovations** (5.0, 50+ reviews, "clean job sites during occupied remodels" — our own positioning), **Carrington Homes**, **Custom Living** (architecture + interior design + construction), **Form Building Company** ("down-to-the-studs"), **Tracy Fisher Design**.

Everything Home has now appeared in **every single query across both research sessions** — 17 of 17.

## Recommendations, ranked

1. **Claim "design-build" explicitly.** We qualify. Add it to the whole-home pillar, the homepage, GBP services, and Houzz. This is the gatekeeper term for the highest-value category we compete in, and adopting it costs nothing because the capability already exists. *Highest leverage on this board.*
2. **Get the Dovetail relationship into third-party sources.** Reciprocal link from Dovetail, Houzz credits naming both, any local coverage. Our own page cannot create an association the model only trusts from outside.
3. **Publish a whole-home price floor.** Still blocked on Eric. This is now demonstrably costing us tier placement, not just a citation.
4. **Rebalance FAQ targeting** toward localized discovery phrasing ("best contractor for X in Fishers") rather than only explainer phrasing.
5. **Re-measure `/working-with-your-designer` in ~30 days** on "contractor who works with interior designers in Fishers Indiana" — we are #3 with a generic description today. If the description changes to mention designer collaboration, the page worked.

## Honest limits
Five queries, one to two runs each — enough to establish direction, not enough to call rank changes. Findings 1, 2 and 3 rest on explicit statements ChatGPT made rather than inference, which is why I weight them heavily. The tiering in Finding 5 came from a single run and should be re-checked.
