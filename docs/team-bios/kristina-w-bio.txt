Kristina brings years of hands-on experience in residential remodeling, having built her background in the industry through owning and operating her own remodeling business. What began with a specialization in tile grew into managing complete kitchen, bathroom, and whole-home renovations, giving her a strong understanding of the construction process, trade coordination, materials, and the many details required to bring a renovation together successfully.

As Director of Operations, Kristina is responsible for helping turn each project from a plan into reality. She works closely with the company’s subcontractors and in-house team to coordinate schedules, manage jobsite operations, troubleshoot challenges, and keep projects moving efficiently from start to finish. Once construction begins, she also serves as a key point of communication for homeowners, helping navigate the day-to-day details and changes that naturally arise throughout a renovation. With a strong appreciation for design, Kristina also enjoys collaborating with the team on design revisions and solutions when projects require adjustments in the field.

Outside of work, Kristina is a proud mom of two boys and loves traveling, trying new restaurants, and spending time with her family. She brings the same energy to her work that she does to life—driven, creative, detail-oriented, and always ready for the next project.
