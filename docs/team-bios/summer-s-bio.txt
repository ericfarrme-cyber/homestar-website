With nearly 10 years of experience in residential design and project coordination, Summer has a passion for creating beautiful, functional spaces that truly feel like home. As a Project Coordinator, she works closely with clients, designers, contractors, and vendors to bring projects from the initial concept through completion. Her experience spans kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, flooring, and whole-home renovations, with a strong focus on organization, communication, attention to detail, and thoughtful design.

Outside of her career, Summer is a proud wife and girl mom who treasures time with her family. Her love for creating beautiful, welcoming spaces extends beyond her work—she believes a home should be a reflection of the people who live there and a place where life’s most meaningful moments happen.
