Eric Farr is the Co-Founder of HomeStar Services & Contracting, a residential remodeling company proudly serving Fishers, Hamilton County, and the greater Indianapolis area. Eric built HomeStar alongside Co-Founder Robb Rice — a friend of more than 20 years — on a simple idea: homeowners deserve a remodeling experience that's transparent, well-managed, and delivers craftsmanship they can count on.

At HomeStar, Eric oversees estimating, project planning, and client relationships, guiding homeowners through everything from kitchen and bathroom remodels to full basement finishes and whole-home renovations. His attention to detail — from scope development to material selections — ensures every project is priced fairly, planned thoroughly, and executed to a high standard.

A seasoned entrepreneur, Eric has founded and operated multiple successful businesses across central Indiana, bringing years of small-business leadership and customer-first thinking to everything he does.

Above all, Eric is a devoted husband and father. Family is at the heart of everything he does — it's why he treats every client's home with the same care he'd give his own. When he's not on a job site, you'll find him staying active with his wife and son or cheering on the Indiana Hoosiers.
