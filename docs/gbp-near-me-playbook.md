## The Core Truth

"Near me" searches are won in the **Google Map Pack** — the 3 businesses shown with a map at the top of results, above all website links. The Map Pack ranking is roughly:

- **~80% Google Business Profile (GBP)**
- **~20% website**

Your website is now best-in-class for Hamilton County basements. This playbook covers the GBP side — where the "near me" battle is actually decided.

Google ranks the Map Pack on **three factors**: **Relevance** (does your profile match the search), **Distance** (how close you are to the searcher), and **Prominence** (how trusted/well-known you are). You can influence all three.

## Priority 1: Review Generation System (Your #1 Near-Me Lever)

Your 5.0 rating across 62+ reviews is elite. But the Map Pack rewards **review volume AND velocity** (recent, steady reviews), not just rating. Competitors like Basement Systems of Indiana have been collecting reviews since 2008. Closing this gap is your single highest-impact near-me action.

### The System (implement this week):
1. **Text every customer a direct review link within 48 hours of project completion** — while the excitement is fresh. SMS gets ~5x the response rate of email.
2. **Use a Google review short link.** Get yours: GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews" → copy the link (looks like `g.page/r/...`). 
3. **Script for your team to text:**
   > "Hi [Name]! It was a pleasure finishing your [Carmel] basement. If you're happy with the work, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It helps other Hamilton County families find us. Takes 60 seconds: [link]. Thank you! — [Your name], HomeStar"
4. **Ask at the moment of delight** — the final walkthrough when they first see the finished space is the perfect time to mention "we'll text you a review link."
5. **Target: 8-12 new reviews/month.** Steady velocity matters more than bursts. Going from 62 → 150+ over the next year with consistent monthly flow will move you up the Map Pack significantly.

### Compliance (don't get penalized):
- ❌ Never offer incentives (discounts, gift cards) for reviews — violates Google policy.
- ❌ Never review-gate (asking only happy customers). Ask everyone.
- ✅ You CAN ask everyone you do business with.
- ✅ Respond to every review (see below).

### Review Responses (you're already doing this — keep going):
Respond to EVERY review with **service + city keywords** baked in naturally. This adds keyword-rich, location-specific content to your profile that feeds both Map Pack and AI search:
> "Thank you so much, [Name]! It was a joy transforming your Westfield basement into the entertainment space your family dreamed of. Enjoy the new bar and theater room! — The HomeStar Team"

Every response should naturally include the **service** (basement finishing) and the **city** (Westfield) when you can.
